PRMUSEUM v5 — DEDICATED TWO-AGENT DISCUSSION SETUP

This version does NOT use transfer_to_agent for AI-to-AI discussion.
AI Discussion opens two independent ElevenLabs conversations at the same time.
The webpage waits for one speaker to finish, relays the completed text to the other,
and changes the portrait only when that specific conversation instance is speaking.

1. CREATE DIALOGUE COPIES IN ELEVENLABS

Create a dialogue copy of each historical agent you want available:
- Edward Bernays — Dialogue
- Ivy Lee — Dialogue
- Walter Lippmann — Dialogue
- Arthur W. Page — Dialogue

The dialogue copy can reuse the same voice, knowledge base, historical/persona prompt,
and pronunciation settings as the visitor-facing version.

REMOVE from each Dialogue agent:
- First Message / greeting (leave it blank)
- transfer_to_agent
- syncActiveAgent
- setActiveAgent
- any prompt instruction that tells the agent to transfer, greet a visitor, or update the webpage

Suggested dialogue-only instruction:

[HISTORICAL ROUNDTABLE MODE]
You are participating in a historical discussion with another historical figure.
You will receive the other speaker's remarks as text.
Respond directly to the argument from your own historical perspective.
Do not greet or address a museum visitor.
Do not attempt to transfer the conversation.
Do not call webpage/client tools.
Keep each turn conversational and reasonably concise.

2. VERCEL VARIABLES

Add the Dialogue agent IDs separately from the visitor agent IDs:

ELEVENLABS_DIALOGUE_AGENT_BERNAYS=agent_...
ELEVENLABS_DIALOGUE_AGENT_IVY_LEE=agent_...
ELEVENLABS_DIALOGUE_AGENT_LIPPMANN=agent_...
ELEVENLABS_DIALOGUE_AGENT_ARTHUR_PAGE=agent_...

Enable whichever ones should appear:
PRMUSEUM_DIALOGUE_ENABLED_AGENTS=bernays,ivy-lee,lippmann,arthur-page

Enable the feature:
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_ENABLED=true
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_MAX_TURNS=6

To hide the feature completely:
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_ENABLED=false

3. HOW IT WORKS

Visitor conversation mode:
- one normal ElevenLabs agent session
- normal visitor microphone/text questions
- visitor-requested transfer behavior can remain on the normal agents

AI Discussion mode:
- two Dialogue agent sessions are opened simultaneously
- both microphones are muted
- Speaker A receives the topic as a text turn
- the app waits until Speaker A has completely stopped speaking
- Speaker A's completed text is sent to Speaker B with sendUserMessage()
- the app waits until Speaker B has completely stopped speaking
- Speaker B's text is sent back to Speaker A
- repeat until PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_MAX_TURNS is reached or the visitor presses Stop

The portrait is tied directly to the session whose onModeChange reports speaking.
No agent transfer or identity inference is involved.

4. ELEVENLABS CONCURRENCY

AI Discussion consumes two simultaneous ElevenLabs conversations.
Your account must support at least two concurrent conversations.
