PRMuseum AI-to-AI Dialogue — v4.4
=================================

This feature does NOT require a new ElevenLabs tool or a new prompt block.
It reuses the app's existing transfer_to_agent behavior.

Vercel
------
Enable:
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_ENABLED=true

Disable entirely:
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_ENABLED=false

Optional maximum autonomous voice turns (2–12):
PRMUSEUM_AI_DIALOGUE_MAX_TURNS=6

ElevenLabs
----------
1. Keep your currently working transfer rules.
2. Each pair of agents you want to use in AI dialogue must be able to transfer to one another.
3. Do not add special broad transfer conditions just for this feature.
4. Keep client events enabled for:
   - audio
   - agent_tool_request
   - agent_tool_response

Behavior
--------
The visitor begins a normal conversation, chooses a second figure and a topic,
and clicks Start dialogue. The current agent speaks first. After each substantive
voice turn, the app issues an internal transfer request to the other selected
agent. The same ElevenLabs conversation and transcript are retained across the
handoff. The microphone is muted while the two figures alternate.

Stop AI dialogue restores the visitor's previous microphone state.
